# V58.74 — Revision-bound Owner-approved 3-Step Action Draft

## Scope and behavior
P1.2 phase: a Plan owner can explicitly prepare a **three-step** action sequence
from a proposed V58.71–73 Draft (self-report, WorkLog, typed Evidence or mixture).
Step 1 reflects the current editable suggested next action. Steps 2/3 are
transparent, Domain-specific generic checkpoints (Study, Development, Career,
fallback). They are NOT AI inference, proven dependencies or a guaranteed plan.

- Creates `plan_action_draft_steps` with unique position within each Draft,
  source `evidence_revision`, editable title and accepted Task reference.
- Preparation is inert: no canonical Task, priority, progress or collaborator
  access is changed. Same-revision prepare retry preserves edited step titles.
- Owner edits all three titles as one scoped form. Only owner may prepare, edit,
  accept or dismiss, even on collaborative Plans.
- The view labels all three as **unapproved**, shows the sequential prerequisite
  rule, and requires explicit bundle acceptance with a fingerprint of the exact
  reviewed titles/order/revision. The old single-Task acceptance remains intact
  for drafts with no prepared steps.
- A new evidence revision (V58.72/73 refresh) makes prepared steps stale:
  they cannot be edited/accepted; owner may explicitly rebuild, warned that
  manually edited step titles will be replaced. No automatic rebuild.
- One transaction locks the Draft and creates exactly 3 todo Tasks, with
  canonical task_dependencies for Step2→Step1 and Step3→Step2, mirrored
  `depends_on_task_id` for older readers. All are within this Plan.
  `accepted_task_id` links every step to its created Task; parent Draft stores
  the first Task for existing compatibility. Retry on accepted Draft is inert.
  Atomic rollback protects against partial import.
- Events for collaborative Plans are recorded within the approval transaction.
  If an approved Task is later deleted, the Draft remains accepted and does not
  recreate it on a retry.
- No external AI calls, cost, auto-scheduling, user-assignment inference,
  or changes to old Tasks. No employer/skill-mastery claims.

## Remaining
This is deliberately a small, linear 3-step proposal, not an AI-authored
multi-level or branching dependency graph. Automatic prioritization, optional
AI cost budgets/fallback, actionable confidence and richer Evidence synthesis
still need validation. P1.2 remains partial. P1.3 spotlight not yet started.
Production and native/PWA E2E remain unverified.
